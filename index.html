The core loop

1
Post

1 to 5 photos: trail cam, harvest, or shed. State, optional county, optional story. Photo location data is stripped.

2
Call it blind

Age class chips (1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.5+) and a gross score slider. You see nothing about other calls until you make yours. One call per buck, ever.

3
Crowd reveal

See the age distribution and score range next to your call.

4
Verify

The owner adds the truth: tape, jawbone or lab, or an official Boone and Crockett / Pope and Young entry. Verified

5
Eye Rating

Points, exact age percentage, and average score miss. Only verified bucks count toward points and leaderboards.

Buck of the Day

One interesting buck, picked daily from the feed, that everyone calls at the same time. Released 6am local, revealed 8pm local. Spoiler free share cards, Wordle style:

🦌 Buck Judge #212 · Age 🟩 · Score 🟨 ±6"

Ranks: earn your eye

Rookie JudgeSpotterField JudgeMaster ScorerHead Judge

Harder bucks weigh more. Verified answers count triple. No size based status for posters, no unverified scores on leaderboards, no fake calls.

Built to protect hunters

No GPS

Location is state and county only, typed by you. Photo metadata is stripped on device and server.

Private by default

Login required beyond this page. Harvest photos are never visible logged out, and share cards use a crop.

No buck shaming

No free text comments at launch. Preset reactions only. First deer and youth posts are celebrate only, with no calls.
